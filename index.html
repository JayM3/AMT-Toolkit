Mathematical Formulation

Scheduled Offer: C = Dsim − R
Target Zero-Out Price:
Ptarget = Paudit + ⅓ ⋅ Paudit ⋅ (1 − C / Dsim)
≡ Paudit ⋅ (1 + ⅓ ⋅ R / Dsim)

In Airlines Manager Tycoon, price elasticity is approximately 3: every 1% price increase reduces passenger demand by ~3%. Therefore, multiplying remaining ratio R / Dsim by 1/3 perfectly zeroes out unsatisfied demand.

Airlines Manager Pro Tips

  • Whole Dollars: In-game ticket prices are integer amounts. The calculator automatically rounds Ptarget so you can paste it directly into your route pricing screen.
  • Negative Remaining Demand: If R < 0 (meaning you over-scheduled aircraft capacity), the price will automatically decrease to stimulate demand and fill all seats.
  • Zero Leftover: When R = 0, Ptarget = Paudit since your current schedule exactly satisfies demand.
