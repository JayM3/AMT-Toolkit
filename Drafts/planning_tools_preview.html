What goes into these numbers?

Transparent assumptions

Each row in the circuit is one round trip per plane per day. Fleet size = the largest route demand ÷ (2 × seats × route repetitions), rounded up.

Daily ticket revenue = the sum of tickets served × that route's saved audit fare. Purchase cost = fleet size × database aircraft price.

Times are recalculated for each aircraft. Aircraft must fit the 24-hour schedule, reach every destination, and meet both airports' category limits.

This is a comparison prototype, not the main Seat Config allocation engine. Aircraft specifications come from the project's existing dataset.
